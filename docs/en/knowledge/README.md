# Knowledge

Curated, durable knowledge that does not belong to a single product or architectural specification is indexed here. Source research remains in `research/`; this section summarizes and links to accepted findings.

The section also governs the project’s known unknowns. [Open Questions](open-questions.md), [Assumptions](assumptions.md), [Constraints](constraints.md), [Risks](risks.md), and the [Decision Log](decision-log.md) are reviewed together so a question, assumption, risk, and accepted decision are never confused with one another.
